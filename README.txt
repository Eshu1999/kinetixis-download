KinetixisMM combines AI models on your Windows PC: it can blend two or more models into one, or keep whichever model does better at your task for each part. It works offline.

REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Free disk space about the size of the model you create.
  An internet connection to sign in (at first, and at least every 14 days).
  Pick the best per task needs enough RAM to load one model (about 4 bytes per parameter on the CPU).

QUICK START
  1. Run KinetixisMM.exe and accept the license agreement on first start.
  2. Sign in with the email and password of your KinetixisMM account (see your purchase email).
     Signing in needs the internet; afterwards the app works offline for up to 14 days at a time.
  3. Wait until the status line says "Ready."
  4. Choose Model A and Model B, pick a method, press "Check compatibility", then "Merge models".
  The result is saved next to Model A. Your original files are never changed.

HELP
  Email arjan@kinetixis.com. If the app cannot start it writes KinetixisMM_error.log next to the program; please attach it.
  If Windows says "unrecognized app", choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
